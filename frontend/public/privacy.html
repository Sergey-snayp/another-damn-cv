Privacy Policy

Another Damn CV and the CV Autofill browser extension · Last updated November 10, 2026

Another Damn CV (anotherdamn.cv) helps you keep a career profile, tailor CVs to job postings and fill job application forms. CV Autofill is its Chrome extension. This page explains what data they handle, why, and who else sees it. In short: your data is used only to provide these features to you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and there are no analytics or tracking tools.

Who runs it

Another Damn CV is run by Sergey Proniuk, an individual developer. Contact: sergeypronyuk@gmail.com.

The extension reads job application forms on the pages you visit in order to recognize fields and fill them. Reading and filling happen inside your browser. Page content is sent to the server only in the cases listed above.

What is stored where

  • On our server: your account, profile, CVs, cover letters, saved jobs and application answers. The server is hosted by Render (United States) and the database by Neon (United States, AWS region us-east-1).
  • In your browser: the extension keeps your sign-in token, settings, a copy of your profile and the CV you chose for filling forms, in Chrome's extension storage on your device.

Who else processes your data

Your data is shared only with the services needed to run the product:

  • Google, for signing in. Google receives your sign-in request, as with any "Sign in with Google" button.
  • Anthropic (Claude), for the AI features. When you use them, the relevant parts of your profile or CV and the job posting are sent to Anthropic to write the result, for example a tailored summary or bullet. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Render and Neon, which host the server and the database.

Your data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising, and is not used to decide creditworthiness or for any purpose unrelated to the features above.

Chrome Web Store user data

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can edit or delete your profile entries, CVs and saved jobs in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and all its data, email sergeypronyuk@gmail.com from the address you sign in with; it will be deleted within 30 days. Signing out of the extension, or removing it, deletes everything it stored in your browser.

Security

All traffic between your browser and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in uses Google, so no password is ever stored. Access to the server and database is limited to the developer.

Children

The service is meant for job seekers and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
